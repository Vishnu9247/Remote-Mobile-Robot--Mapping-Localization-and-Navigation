HX-30HM MOBILE BASE R1 - ENGINEERING PROTOTYPE

1. Read docs/HX30HM_R1_Build_Guide.pdf.
2. Open STEP/ASSEMBLY_HX30HM_R1.step in Fusion or another STEP-capable CAD app.
3. Individual printed parts are in STEP/ and bed-oriented print meshes in STL/.
4. Manufacturer servo/horn STEP references are in reference/.
5. source/build_robot.py reproduces the parts and assembly using CadQuery 2.x.
   Dimensions are editable in this script. nominal_dimensions.json is a summary.
6. docs/validation.json contains solid, fit, assembly reimport and STL checks.

Print P12 first, then one P03/P04/P07 servo fit set and shims. Confirm actual
horn revision, bolt circle, threads, screw depth and housing fit before
printing the full set. Follow the guide for material, quantities and hardware.

The 3 kg figure is a total-mass design target, not a proven rating. The package
has not been physically printed or load-tested. Production release is pending
fit testing, printed-joint qualification and confirmation of servo radial loads.

Assembly includes nominal unthreaded standard fasteners. OEM horn centre
retaining screws are required and specified but not modeled. Sensor-specific
mounts, electronics and battery are not included: mounting grids and straps
are provided for those later selections. No native Fusion motion joints are set.
